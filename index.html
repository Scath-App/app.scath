Simple Onboarding

Up and running in
under 3 minutes

No paperwork. No branch visits. No stress. Just download, verify, and you're banking.

1

Download & Sign Up

Get Scath from the Play Store or App Store. Create your account with just your name, phone number, and BVN. Your data is encrypted end-to-end from the first second.

2

Verify Your Identity

Complete our quick KYC in under 60 seconds — snap your ID and a selfie. Biometric authentication (Face ID / fingerprint) keeps your account locked to you alone.

3

Fund & Start Banking

Add money via bank transfer or card. Start sending, investing, splitting bills, and earning rewards immediately. Your Scath account number is ready instantly.
